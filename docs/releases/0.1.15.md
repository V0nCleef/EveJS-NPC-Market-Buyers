NPCs buy your sell orders in sensible, random batches, as long as the price is fair. NPC Market Buyers works with the EveJS Launcher and provides its own in-game window.

[![Watch the showcase](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/V0nCleef/EveJS-NPC-Market-Buyers/main/docs/media/showcase-thumbnail.jpg)](https://github.com/V0nCleef/EveJS-NPC-Market-Buyers/releases/download/v0.1.15/NPC-Market-Buyers-Showcase-0.1.15.mp4)

[Watch the showcase](https://github.com/V0nCleef/EveJS-NPC-Market-Buyers/releases/download/v0.1.15/NPC-Market-Buyers-Showcase-0.1.15.mp4) — English LYRA narration and captions. The video covers opening the window, selling, pricing choices, automatic price backups and the main settings.

- Price advice, buying chance and possible batch size appear in the normal **Sell Items** window.
- Choose **TQ trade hub** prices or **EVE average**. Missing or rejected unrealistic hub prices fall back to EVE average, then an eligible local NPC buy offer. EVE average is EVE Online's overall average; local NPC offers are a separate backup.
- **Unlimited** removes spending caps and item-volume quotas. **Limited** adds budgets and quotas. Both require fair prices.
- **My Orders** shows original and remaining quantities, with bars that drain as stock sells. My Orders and Activity offer **Icons / Details / List** views.
- Optional **offline purchases** catch up after a restart. Wallet income is bundled, while Activity keeps each sale.
- Clear help popups, individual field resets, readable Wallet descriptions and eight client languages are included.

Requires Native EveJS **0.12.9**, Launcher **1.0.69+** and EVE client build **3396210**.

Stop EveJS and the native market, and close EVE. Import **NPC-Market-Buyers-0.1.15.zip** through **Launcher → Mods → Add ZIP**, enable it, then restart and reconnect. Updating users must disable/remove the old package first; keep only one version enabled. Saved policy and purchase history are preserved.

Open **Mods → NPC Market Buyers** in game. Download the attached Launcher ZIP for installation. GitHub's automatic **Source code** downloads are development archives. `SHA256SUMS.txt` identifies the reviewed mod package.

The maintainer tested and approved the mod in game, then separately approved the showcase and publication. See [Verification](https://github.com/V0nCleef/EveJS-NPC-Market-Buyers/blob/main/VERIFICATION.md) for automated checks and their limits.
